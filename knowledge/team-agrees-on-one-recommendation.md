# The whole team must recommend the same approach, because we build it together

In Lecture 12 the instructor said that even if reports are written separately, the team must agree on one answer to "RNN or not?", because the team implements it together. His example: if the team did all its work in Accra, your report can't then say the capital is Lagos. "Either you didn't help the team or they were all wrong and you were right."

**For us:** the recommendation comes from the shared results table (M1–M4 on the same test set), agreed as a decision record before anyone writes Section A.
