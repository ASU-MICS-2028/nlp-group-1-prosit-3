# Cite results and others' claims; general knowledge in your own words needs no citation

From the instructor (Lecture 4):
- Describing a well-known concept in your own words (what an RNN is) needs **no citation**.
- Citing someone's **results or specific claims** ("Whisper reaches X% WER on…") **does** need one.
- **Someone's exact words** go in quotation marks, with a citation.
- Not every sentence needs a citation.

In ACL style, cite as "(Radford et al., 2023)" or "Radford et al. (2023) showed…" ([[report-format]]).
