# Ad hoc retrieval has changing queries over fixed documents; filtering has a fixed query over changing documents

**Information retrieval (IR)** means retrieving any media to meet a user's information need. A **document** is anything indexed and searched (a page, a paper, even a sentence), and a **collection** or corpus is a set of documents.

- **Ad hoc retrieval:** the documents stay fixed and the queries change. The course focuses on this.
- **Information filtering:** the query stays fixed and the documents change.

A **factoid question** asks for one specific fact with no opinion ("What is the capital of Ghana?").

**Example:** staff asking many different questions about a fixed set of company policies is ad hoc retrieval. A news alert that always watches for "cholera outbreak" as new articles arrive is filtering.
