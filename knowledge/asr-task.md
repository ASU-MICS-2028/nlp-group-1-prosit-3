# Automatic speech recognition turns a waveform into text, and the use decides the design

ASR takes **audio in and gives text out**. The instructor said to frame any deep learning problem by its input and output: speech-to-text, text-to-speech, image-to-text.

**The use decides the design:**
- A voice assistant must transcribe, work out the intent and act.
- A courtroom tool only transcribes (and maybe summarises).
- Speech translation (speech in one language, text in another) is a different problem.

**Ayamra's case:** dictation. Long-form clinical speech from one speaker, transcribed word for word into notes. Accuracy on medical words matters more than speed of response.
